官方题目PDFDMOJ

题目在问什么

已知最小孩子和中间孩子的年龄，按相同的年龄差推算最大孩子的年龄。

考查知识点

  • 等差数列：相邻两项之间的差相同。例如 5、10、15 每次增加 5。
  • 年龄差：中间孩子比最小孩子大 M−Y 岁，其中 Y 是最小年龄，M 是中间年龄。
  • 代入公式：最大年龄等于中间年龄再加一次相同的差。

一步一步想

  1. 读取最小孩子年龄 Y 和中间孩子年龄 M。题目保证 Y 不大于 M。
  2. 计算相邻年龄差 gap = M - Y。
  3. 用 M + gap 计算最大孩子年龄并输出。把 gap 展开后也可写成 2 × M - Y。

用例子走一遍

输入 Y=12、M=15 时，年龄差是 15-12=3。最大孩子比中间孩子大 3 岁，所以年龄是 15+3=18。

为什么这样做是对的

题目保证三个年龄构成等差数列，因此中间到最大的年龄差，必须等于最小到中间的差 M-Y。程序输出 M+(M-Y)，正好是在中间年龄上加上这个差，得到符合规则的最大年龄。

运行时间与内存

程序只读取两个数并进行固定次数的加减乘法，所以运行时间是 O(1)。O(1) 表示输入年龄变大时，运算次数不增加。额外空间也是 O(1)，只使用固定数量的整数。

容易出错的地方

  • 要把年龄差加到中间孩子的年龄上；不要重复输出中间年龄或减去差值。
  • 最小和中间年龄相同是合法情况，此时差为 0，最大年龄也相同。
  • 输入年龄最多为 50，但推算出的最大年龄可以是 100；不要把答案限制在 50 以内。

Python 3 解法

solution.py
import sys

youngest, middle = map(int, sys.stdin.read().split())
print(2 * middle - youngest)

Problem statement (English)
Problem J1: Next in line
Problem Description
You know a family with three children. Their ages form an arithmetic sequence: the difference in
ages between the middle child and youngest child is the same as the difference in ages between
the oldest child and the middle child. For example, their ages could be 5, 10 and 15, since both
adjacent pairs have a difference of 5 years.
Given the ages of the youngest and middle children, what is the age of the oldest child?

Input Specification
The input consists of two integers, each on a separate line. The first line is the age Y of the
youngest child (0 ≤ Y ≤ 50). The second line is the age M of the middle child (Y ≤ M ≤ 50).

Output Specification
The output will be the age of the oldest child.

Sample Input 1
12
15

Output for Sample Input 1
18

Sample Input 2
10
10

Output for Sample Input 2
10

Official problemPDFDMOJ

What are we asked to do?

Given the youngest and middle children's ages, use the same age difference once more to find the oldest child's age.

Knowledge and skills

  • Arithmetic sequence: the difference between each pair of neighbours is the same. For example, 5, 10, 15 increases by 5 each time.
  • Age difference: the middle child is M−Y years older than the youngest, where Y is the youngest age and M is the middle age.
  • Substitution: the oldest age is the middle age plus that same difference.

Think step by step

  1. Read the youngest age Y and the middle age M. The statement guarantees that Y is at most M.
  2. Calculate the gap = M - Y between the two given ages.
  3. Calculate and print M + gap for the oldest age. Expanding the gap also gives 2 × M - Y.

Walk through an example

For Y=12 and M=15, the gap is 15-12=3. The oldest child is 3 years older than the middle child, so the age is 15+3=18.

Why does this work?

The three ages form an arithmetic sequence, so the difference from the middle child to the oldest must equal the difference from the youngest to the middle: M-Y. The program prints M+(M-Y), which adds exactly that difference to the middle age and therefore gives the required oldest age.

Time and memory

The program reads two numbers and performs a fixed number of additions, subtractions, and multiplications, so its running time is O(1). O(1) means the number of operations does not grow with the ages. Extra space is also O(1), since only a fixed number of integers are used.

Common mistakes

  • Add the age gap to the middle child's age; do not repeat the middle age or subtract the gap.
  • Equal youngest and middle ages are allowed. Their gap is 0, so the oldest age is equal too.
  • The input ages are at most 50, but the calculated oldest age can be 100; do not cap the answer at 50.

Python 3 solution

solution.py
import sys

youngest, middle = map(int, sys.stdin.read().split())
print(2 * middle - youngest)
